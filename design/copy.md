# Copy (draft-2)

Tone: **honest, never punishing**. State facts, then offer a next step. The UI ships in **English (base) and Russian** (see `i18n.md`); user data (line names, tasks, notes) is shown as is, in any script.

## Banned words for intentions, lines and plans

- **EN:** overdue · failed · behind · late · missed · streak broken · you should · warning, and any red styling.
- **RU:** просрочено · провал · провалено · отстаёшь / отставание · опоздал · пропущено · серия прервана · ты должен / нужно было · предупреждение.

A copy-review test greps the String Catalog in every language for these words. Exception: "Due" / «Срок» is allowed **only** for a real external due date. The amber `Due` badge is used only for a deadline under the deadline rule (`screens/settings.md` § Deadlines); any other due date reads `due 20:35` in plain text.

## Formatting

- Dates: `Thursday, 1 October` / `четверг, 1 октября`; compact `Thu 1 Oct` / `чт 1 окт`; ISO week `Week 40` / `W40` · `Неделя 40` / `Н40`.
- Durations: `≈ 20 min`, `≈ 1 h 30 min` (never "1.5 h" for tasks). Budgets may use decimals: `4.5 h/week` / `4,5 ч/нед` (locale decimal separator).
- Plurals always through the String Catalog (RU: one / few / many / other).
- Trace chip: `Project → 🎻 Line → Artifact`.

## My take strings (draft-4)

The take is the owner's own word about his week. The app repeats it and never rephrases it: no "regress", "worse", "declined", "bad week", "only" / «хуже», «откат», «регресс», «плохая неделя», «всего лишь». Where measured facts and a take stand together they are joined by `·` — never by "but", "despite", "although" / «но», «несмотря на», «хотя».

**Take history, not journal (owner, 2026-10-09).** The list of takes and notes in Line detail is the **take history** (component `TakeHistoryRow`); "journal" means only the daily and weekly notes of ADR-0015.

**Names — decided (owner, 3 Oct 2026).** Feature: EN `My take`, RU **`Мой взгляд`** (not «Моя оценка»: «оценка» already means _estimate_ in this UI). Directions: `Leap · Rise · Steady · Dip` / `Рывок · Подъём · Ровно · Спуск`; Rise and Dip replace Forward / «Вперёд» and Back / «Назад». No `My take:` prefix is needed for accessibility.
