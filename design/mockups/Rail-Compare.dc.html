Decided: direction 1, for every day (owner, 2026-11-17)

  • One layout for every day. It is the hybrid’s dense mode, and on the sparse Thursday it reads like the proportional rail: gaps up to an hour are to scale, longer ones are labelled. The rail never changes its look because one task was added.
  • Titles get the full width. No columns, no “+N”. Measured on these artboards at 300 pt: Monday keeps every title whole except the nested span with its range (15 of 16 characters); Thursday keeps 17–28. In the emulated build the column pairs keep 7–9 letters (3–5 in the real build, as reported).
  • Time stays where the eye starts: one gutter, same x on every row.
  • Height from 08:00 to 22:00: Monday 525 pt, Thursday 395 pt; the proportional rail is 364 pt but does not hold the dense day.
  • Direction 2 reads well as a list, but the bar and the rows do not line up, so the day’s shape needs a hover; it is close to the agenda the owner turned down on 2 Oct (variant A).
  • Direction 3 costs two layouts, a threshold to test and a rail that looks different from day to day.

Owner findings, 2026-11-20

  • Two lanes (phase 2): one compressed axis for Plan and Tracked; a gap collapses only where both lanes are empty; rows stay aligned across the lanes.
  • The now line: in a gap by proportion; between rows at the boundary after the last row that started at or before now; inside an ongoing span a dot on its duration line. The label stays in the gutter.
  • Nested spans: only the outer duration line; the inner span shows its own range as secondary text.
