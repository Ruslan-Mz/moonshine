Recommendation: ship direction 1

  • One layout for every day. It is the hybrid’s dense mode, and on the sparse Thursday it reads like the proportional rail: gaps up to an hour are to scale, longer ones are labelled. The rail never changes its look because one task was added at 08:50.
  • Titles get the full width. No columns, no “+2”. Measured on these artboards: at 300 pt only a due row truncates on Tuesday (17 of 19 characters stay); at 213 pt in Russian every title keeps 12–19 characters. The emulated build keeps 7–9 letters in its column pairs (3–5 in the real build, as reported) and hides two tasks behind “+2”.
  • Time stays where the eye starts: one gutter, same x on every row.
  • Shorter than the problem it solves. Tuesday is 442 pt; the proportional rail is 364 pt but unreadable.
  • Direction 2 reads well as a list at 300 pt, but at 213 pt the day bar and the time column leave 3–9 characters of title. It is also close to the agenda the owner turned down on 2 Oct (variant A); the bar and the rows do not line up, so the day’s shape needs a hover.
  • Direction 3 costs two layouts, a threshold to test and a rail that looks different from day to day, for a gain only on sparse days, where direction 1 already looks almost the same.

What it costs

  • A layout pass in Core (pure function: items → rows, gaps, duration lines), testable without UI.
  • Absolute position is no longer exact across gaps: the gap label carries the range, and the now line is placed inside the gap by proportion.
