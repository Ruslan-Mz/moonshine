## Key flows

- **Sync-in (phase 1):** providers produce snapshots → `Store` upserts by `ExternalRef` → Services read only from `Store`. Completed tasks outlive the import window; other missing items are soft-deleted (ADR-0002), except lines, which are soft-deleted only after a clean vault read (2026-10-07), and notes and artifacts, which are soft-deleted only when the read saw that they are gone: one the read could not see or no longer recognises stays as stored (2026-10-10). Imports are single-flight per service, and a commit names the store version its identity map was read at (stale → `IdentityConflict`). UI is fast and works offline; providers refresh on launch / ⌘R / timer.
- **Commands (phase 2 for native data; the Write phase for the vault; the Act phase for OmniFocus and calendars):** UI or assistant emits `Command` (e.g. `LogTime`, `AddTakeNote`; later `CompleteTask(id)`) → `CommandBus` → every command lands in the activity log with undo. Phase 2 introduces the bus for MoonShine-native writes (time entries and their captures, timer sessions, capacity exceptions, plans; takes come with My take, cut from phase 2 on 2026-11-01); the Reminders slice adds the reminder commands (ADR-0017); P3 adds the memo commands (ADR-0018). In the Act phase the bus checks provider **capabilities** → adapter executes → Store refreshed; a provider command created on another device is queued and executed by the Mac (ADR-0013).
- **Vault commands (the Write phase):** UI or assistant emits a vault command (`CreateNote`, `AppendToNote`, `UpdateNoteBody`, `SetNoteProperty`) → `CommandBus` → `VaultWriter` in the `ObsidianAdapter`: role folder check, precondition (hash of the content it was based on; mismatch → nothing written, both versions shown), atomic replace, byte fidelity, previous content kept 30 days for undo. Only the Mac writes; a vault command from the iPad or iPhone is queued under the once-only rules of ADR-0013. A ritual renders an Obsidian template into a draft; nothing is written before the owner saves (ADR-0015).
- **Timer (phase 2, Timer slice):** `StartTimer` (a plain start stops every running session at that instant and offers `Keep running` for about eight seconds, which restores them within the cap; ⌥-start runs alongside; up to three at once; with three running, an alongside start stops the oldest, a plain start stops all three, and the notice "stopped (three at most)" has no button) → `TimerSession` → each interval between start, pause, resume and stop becomes a `TimeEntry` (source `timer`; split at local midnight) → edits through `EditTimeEntry` / `SplitTimeEntry` with undo → the import reconciles native and `excel` entries by (day, line): within 10 minutes the pair resolves as "the same session" and can be reopened from the day view, beyond it the pair goes to the reconcile list; the owner's choice is stored, and no entry is ever negative (ADR-0016). Lines' hours count their own entries in full; the day's clock time is the union of intervals and never exceeds 24 h; "in parallel" is tracked minus by the clock for the day, and for a line the part of its time overlapped by at least one other session.
- **Notifications (from whichever of the Timer or Reminders slice ships first):** `NotificationPlanner` in Core reads reminders (with `snoozedUntil`), `ReminderSeen`, running timers and, from P2, memo moments (ADR-0018) and decides what to schedule → the per-device `Notifier` adapter schedules local notifications. On the Mac a recurring reminder becomes repeating calendar triggers, which macOS fires with the app closed; MoonShine runs as a login item and refills on launch, on wake and whenever a reminder changes (no background refresh on the Mac). On the iPad and iPhone the planner schedules the next 24 hours (at most 64 pending; a running timer's nudges rank first, then reminders by time; what does not fit waits for the next refill; a reminder that does not fit is marked "notifications limited on this device") and refills on launch, on wake, on background refresh and whenever a reminder changes. The app also writes the widget snapshot (current reminders, a few next tasks; no event titles) into an App Group container. A reminder seen or archived on one device removes its pending notifications on the others when the change arrives (ADR-0017). In phase 1 (S4) only the tag bridge exists: OmniFocus tasks with the reminder tag are shown in Today's Reminders strip and hidden from every task list and count; notifications stay with OmniFocus.
- **Take (phase 2):** the owner's judgement of a line's week. Rhythm, progress and take are three tracks, never summed; a take does not change forecasts.
- **Forecast (phase 2–3):** evidence → Calibration (bias, adherence per line) → ForecastEngine simulates the scenario over Capacity → bands per step/project → Planner view models; weekly job stores ForecastSnapshots.
- **Assistant (phase 4):** conversation → `AIProvider` with tool schemas from `ToolRegistry` → read tools execute immediately (note bodies only from the folders on the assistant's allowlist, ADR-0015); write tools return a _proposal_ (a `Command` preview) the user confirms. From phase 4 a voice `Capture` becomes `auto` commands (applied at once) or a `Proposal` the owner confirms. Until the Act phase `auto` covers native commands (log time, add a take note, start, pause, resume and stop a timer, edit the entry just stopped, ADR-0016; create, edit, mark seen, snooze, archive and convert a reminder, ADR-0017; record a memo outcome, choose another variant, dismiss a memo, ADR-0018) and one provider command, `AppendJournal` (ADR-0015); from the Act phase it also covers `CompleteTask` and `CreateTask` (inbox), executed through the queue. Provider changes in a proposal are shown as a checklist with deep links until the Act phase; a failed or unresolved `auto` command lands in the review inbox (ADR-0013).
