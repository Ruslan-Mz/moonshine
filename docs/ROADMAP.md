## Why iPad in 3b, not phase 7

The owner plans on the iPad (OmniFocus is good for doing, poor for planning). Once Plan and Board exist, the iPad is where their value is used. Sync is limited to what 3b needs: the Mac remains the only importer; the iPad and iPhone write only MoonShine-native data (ADR-0010); a vault command created there is queued and executed by the Mac (ADR-0015).

Phase 1's timebox was raised from 2 to 3 weekends for the calendar and the day rail (decision 2026-10-02).

## Why Write before the iPad

The iPad and iPhone are designed once, with the journal and the rituals in them, instead of being redesigned after a later notes phase. Write comes after Board so that the planning core (Plan, Board) is not delayed by a commodity editor: MoonShine's editor stays plain and Obsidian remains the editor for long texts (decision 2026-10-06, ADR-0015).

## Why Act directly after Ask

The owner wants the assistant as a secretary: dictated facts recorded at once, plan changes confirmed later (ADR-0013). Ask produces the proposals; Act lets confirmed proposals reach OmniFocus and the vault. Zoom out follows them (decision 2026-10-03).

## Why Plan before Ask

The planning engine is the part that helps plans actually get done, and it is MoonShine-native (no provider limits). The assistant is far more useful once it can reason over plans and scenarios ("what if violin goes to max?"). Measurement showcase comes from the engine's tests (seeded simulations, property tests) as well as from later evals.

## Gates for 7–8 (from the owner's own rules)

- Phase 7: daily driver for ≥ 2 months **and** a concrete OmniFocus/Obsidian limitation blocks me.
- Phase 8: ≥ 50 people outside my circle asked for it (blog/GitHub), **and** at least one said "I'd pay" — then name a price immediately.
